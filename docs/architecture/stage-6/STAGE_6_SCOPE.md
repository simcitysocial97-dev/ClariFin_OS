# Stage 6 — Financial Intelligence Engine

## Objective

Build the deterministic reasoning layer for ClariFin_OS.

The Intelligence Engine transforms financial data and graph relationships into explainable financial intelligence.

This stage does not introduce LLM reasoning.

Every conclusion must be reproducible from financial data.

---

## Responsibilities

The Intelligence Engine generates:

- financial health metrics
- spending intelligence
- cashflow intelligence
- debt intelligence
- investment intelligence
- behavioural intelligence
- anomalies
- opportunities
- risks
- goals
- recommendations

Every result must include explainability.

---

## Consumes

Financial Graph Runtime

Command Center Runtime

Workspace capabilities

No direct UI logic.

---

## Produces

Insights

Alerts

Recommendations

Risk scores

Opportunity scores

Goal tracking

Evidence chains

---

## Out of Scope

Chatbot

Natural language

Prompt engineering

LLMs

Voice assistant

Automation

---

## Exit Criteria

Every financial insight is deterministic.

Every recommendation is explainable.

Every score is reproducible.

No black-box intelligence exists.
